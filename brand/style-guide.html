Brand Architecture

FleetBrain — the company, the methodology, the consulting brand. Professional, trustworthy, systematic.

StanleyBot — the product, the AI agent platform. Friendly, approachable, competent.

Both share the same color system but with different weight. FleetBrain leans into dark backgrounds and cyan accents. StanleyBot leads with coral and the Stan character.

Stan — The Mascot

Stan is a coral-red octopus with two mechanical claws and glowing cyan eyes. Friendly, competent, not intimidating.

Character Specifications

SpeciesOctopus (stylized)
Body colorCoral gradient (#FF7A5C → #C44835)
EyesLarge, dark sclera (#0A0A12), cyan glow pupils (#00FFFF)
ClawsTwo front-facing open pincers, dark mechanical joints (#2A2A35)
Tentacles4 visible, rounded, darker coral (#B83D2E)
ExpressionSubtle smile — warm, not goofy
Style3D stylized clay render, Pixar quality, vinyl toy aesthetic

Variants

Hero / Full Body

Front-facing, claws raised. Landing pages, hero sections, about pages.

Peeking

Stan peeking from bottom edge, claws gripping. Headers, banners, social covers.

Flat Icon

Simplified SVG silhouette. Favicons, app icons, small UI contexts.

Action Poses

Stan holding phone/tablet, waving, working. Feature illustrations, onboarding.

Brand Voice

Target audience: Service company owners who need AI but lack technical expertise.

DO

"Stan handles your scheduling so you don't have to."

"Gets smarter every time your team corrects it."

"Your AI ops — managed, not DIY."

Direct. Competent. Warm. Matter-of-fact.

DON'T

"Revolutionary AI-powered next-gen solution!"

"Leverage cutting-edge machine learning..."

"Unlock the power of artificial intelligence!"

No hype. No jargon. No buzzwords. No selling.

Core Message

Stan's value comes from accumulated human corrections, not technology. Each engagement makes the system better. Compounding intelligence, not just another chatbot.
